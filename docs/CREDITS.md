# Credits and license scope

SSNE-Native-Multiplayer is an unofficial community fork for Serious Sam: Next
Encounter (GameCube). It is based on:

- [Silly ReEncounter](https://github.com/takoshero96/Silly-ReEncounter), by takoshero96.
- [SSNE-Native](https://github.com/SashexSRB/SSNE-Native), including the 1.2.7 application baseline.
- SDL2, Dear ImGui, Erica One, Outfit and the LLVM/MinGW toolchain. Their notices
  and full license texts accompany the downloadable Windows package.

[LICENSE.txt](../LICENSE.txt) retains the original project's license and its
scope. Third-party components keep their own licenses. Serious Sam: Next
Encounter, its media, original executable and other game content belong to their
respective owners and are outside that license dedication.

The release provides host tools/runtime objects and a sparse PAL patch recipe.
Players extract and translate their own supported disc locally; complete generated
game sources, compiled game executables, saves and disc/media files are not shipped.
Source/package scans check accidental inclusion, not legal provenance or rights clearance.
